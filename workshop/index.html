CMMC Level 1 readiness: what the owner and IT provider each need to do

A proposed free, 45-minute educational session for small defense subcontractors. Express interest to receive the proposed session details. This is not an assessment or certification, and no date has been announced yet.

What to send when you express interest

Business name, work email, your role, and whether the company has a current Level 1 requirement or question. Employee band is optional. Do not send attachments, contract documents, CUI, credentials, device inventories or sensitive technical descriptions.
