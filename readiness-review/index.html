How an introduction works

Tell us what prompted the request

Share which bid or renewal is involved, the kind of help you need, and when you need an answer. A short description is enough.

Choose whether to connect

We check whether a firm covers that work and has capacity. You decide whether we share your contact details with that firm.

Work directly with the provider

The firm discusses your requirements, quotes its own fees and delivers the work under its agreement with you. Onyx may earn a referral fee if you hire it.

What to include

Example from a fictional supplier

“A customer asked us for a cybersecurity questionnaire before our December renewal. Our IT provider handles the systems. We need someone to review the documentation.”

A business contact and a short description are enough. Keep contract documents, controlled information and credentials out of the inquiry.
