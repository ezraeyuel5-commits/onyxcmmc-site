FAQ

Common questions

Does Level 1 apply to my company?

Level 1 applies when you handle Federal Contract Information on a DoD contract or subcontract. We confirm this on the fit check by looking at your work and your contract language.

What if we receive CUI, like controlled drawings or specs?

Then you likely need Level 2, which is a larger project. We'll tell you on the fit check and refer you to a qualified Level 2 partner.

Do you replace our IT person or provider?

No. Your IT stays responsible for the technical side. We handle the written side: policies, evidence and the affirmation, and we coordinate with your IT where needed.

Is this a certification?

Level 1 is a self-assessment that your company affirms in SPRS. We prepare you and your evidence so the affirmation is accurate and supported. We don't issue certifications, and no one can guarantee a contract award.

Our prime asked for our "SPRS score." What do we send?

Level 1 has no score, only met or not met. Primes can't see your SPRS record directly, so the usual proof is a PDF of your affirmed Level 1 record, which we help you produce.
