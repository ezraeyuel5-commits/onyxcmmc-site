FAQ

Common questions

Does Level 1 apply to my company?

The relevant information, systems and contract requirements determine the scope—not employee count alone. We discuss fit first and identify what needs further review before setup.

What if we receive CUI, like controlled drawings or specs?

Level 2/CUI work is excluded from this offer. We identify that scope limitation and discuss an appropriate separate provider; we do not ask you to upload CUI to this site.

Do you replace our IT person or provider?

No. Your technical provider stays responsible for agreed technical changes and ongoing support. We coordinate preparation and evidence within scope; your senior official owns the affirmation.

Is this a certification?

Onyx provides preparation and guidance, not official certification. The client reviews the underlying assessment and makes the affirmation. We do not guarantee compliance outcomes, acceptance or contract awards.

Our prime asked for our "SPRS score." What do we send?

Ask which specific assessment or record the request concerns before sending anything. We can help organize the relevant request and record within scope; do not post contract documents or account credentials in a public inquiry.
