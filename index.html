How an introduction works

Tell us what prompted the request

Share which bid or renewal is involved, the kind of help you need, and when you need an answer. A short description is enough.

Choose whether to connect

We check whether a firm covers that work and has capacity. You decide whether we share your contact details with that firm.

Work directly with the provider

The firm discusses your requirements, quotes its own fees and delivers the work under its agreement with you. Onyx may earn a referral fee if you hire it.

For CMMC service firms

Onyx focuses on customer acquisition. We look for defense suppliers with an active need for outside help and ask whether they want an introduction. Your team owns technical discovery, the sale and delivery.

To discuss a referral relationship, tell us which suppliers you serve, the work you take on and how you register and report referred business.

The Level 1 checklist

The 15 safeguarding requirements from FAR 52.204-21, in plain English, with what you'd show as proof. Check each one you could prove today.

#RequirementWhat you'd show
1Only authorized users, processes and devices access covered systemsA current list of users and company devices
2People can only do what their role needsWho has admin rights, and why
3Control connections to outside systemsDocumented rules and evidence of implemented connection controls
4Control information on publicly accessible systemsWho approves website and social posts
5Identify users, processes acting for users, and devicesAccount and device identifiers tied to authorized use
6Verify identity before accessPassword or sign-in settings
7Wipe or destroy media before disposal or reuseDisposal log or shredding receipts
8Limit physical access to equipment and work areasLocked areas and who has keys
9Escort visitors, log visits, manage keys and badgesVisitor log and key list
10Protect external and key internal communication boundariesFirewall or router settings
11Keep public-facing systems separate from internal onesEvidence of physical or logical separation for public-facing system components
12Identify, report and correct security flaws promptlyUpdate and patch history
13Malicious-code protection is in place where appropriateProtection coverage for the relevant in-scope environment
14Antivirus stays updatedAuto-update turned on
15Scan periodically and scan external files when downloaded, opened or executedScan settings or a recent scan report

Illustrative evidence examples—not a complete assessment, mandatory document list or proof of compliance. Source: FAR 52.204-21(b)(1). General guidance, not legal advice.
