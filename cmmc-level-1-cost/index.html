The short answer

Level 1 is a company self-assessment, not a required third-party certification assessment. Outside preparation help is optional. Your total cost can include staff time, chosen consulting support, and the technical work needed to implement safeguards. See the official program overview and 32 CFR 170.15 in the published CMMC rule.

The October 15, 2024 final rule modeled approximately $5,977 per small entity for a Level 1 self-assessment and initial affirmation. That model includes a $560 initial-affirmation component; it is not $5,977 plus another $560 for the same initial affirmation. The rule separately modeled $560 for a reaffirmation. These are historical regulatory burden estimates based on assumed labor—not current consultant quotes or observed amounts paid by Onyx customers. Source: final rule, “CMMC Level 1 Self-Assessment and Affirmation Costs,” Small Entities.

The model assumed existing safeguarding obligations had already been implemented. It should not be used as an all-in budget for a business that still needs technical changes.

Three ways to approach the work

Do it yourself

Use the official requirements and assessment guidance, involve your IT owner, and retain support for your assessment. There is no requirement to buy Onyx’s service or a policy template pack.

Defined consulting engagement

Buy a written scope with named deliverables, responsibilities, exclusions and an end point. Ask separately who implements technical fixes.

Managed IT support

Compare the actual provider proposal. Ongoing IT operations and a readiness engagement are different scopes; do not assume one includes the other.

What changes the scope and total cost

  • Systems and information: establish where FCI is processed, stored or transmitted; headcount alone does not define the assessment boundary.
  • Starting condition: functioning antivirus, a firewall and individual accounts do not by themselves establish all requirements are met.
  • Technical work: identify each change, who is authorized to make it and its separate cost before committing.
  • Locations and devices: agree which assets and operating environments are included.
  • Evidence quality: identify what exists, what is missing and what needs testing. Documents must reflect real practices.
  • CUI or a higher-level requirement: stop and rescope rather than squeezing it into a Level 1 package.

Free sources before you purchase

Read FAR 52.204-21, the official CMMC scoping and assessment resources, and SPRS entry and affirmation guidance. Common questions

Is the $495 paid in addition to setup?

No. It is credited toward the setup total: $3,005 remains on the $3,500 tier or $4,005 on the $4,500 tier after the review is paid, under the agreed engagement terms.

Is the $750 annual review mandatory?

No. It is an optional Onyx service requiring separate authorization. The customer’s ongoing safeguards and applicable assessment/affirmation obligations are separate from buying our renewal.

Is there a mandatory outside-assessor fee for Level 1?

No. Level 1 uses self-assessment. You may hire preparation support, but it does not transfer your company’s assessment or affirmation responsibilities.

Does a policy pack make us compliant?

No. A policy is not proof of implementation. Onyx’s documentation and evidence-organization work is a service deliverable, not a mandatory government-purchased template or a guarantee of compliance.
