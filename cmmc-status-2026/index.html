What the official announcement says

Source-checked on October 29, 2026

The July 13 official announcement states that Phase II requirements originally scheduled for November 10, 2026 were suspended. It also states that Phase I self-assessment requirements remain in place and that a program review was being initiated.

This page does not assert a new restart date, that a task-force report is or is not publicly available, or that every existing contract has already been modified. Those details require their own current evidence. Use the official CMMC overview and the notices applicable to your procurement.

What a Level 1 company should check

  1. Contract: identify the actual CMMC level and assessment type, applicable clause or flowdown, and any amendment or deviation affecting this procurement.
  2. Information: distinguish FCI from CUI and identify the relevant systems; employee count is not the deciding factor.
  3. Implementation: maintain applicable safeguards. A rollout pause is not a blanket release from contractual cybersecurity duties.
  4. Assessment and affirmation: when Level 1 applies, follow the annual self-assessment and affirmation requirements with supported results.
  5. Uncertainty: resolve disputed or unclear language with the contracting officer, prime or qualified counsel before relying on a general article.

FAR 52.204-21 contains the basic safeguarding requirements. 32 CFR 170.22 sets out affirmation responsibilities. Neither an Onyx guide nor a website checklist changes your contract.

Onyx does not guarantee government acceptance, contract eligibility, a contract award or a compliance outcome. This page is not a determination of what a particular solicitation requires. It does not cover Level 2/CUI implementation, legal advice or ongoing IT operations.

Common questions

Did the announcement cancel all CMMC requirements?

No. The official July 13 announcement retained Phase I self-assessment requirements while suspending Phase II and initiating a review. Check the current official guidance and your contract.

Does the pause mean we can ignore a Level 1 requirement?

No. Do not disregard an applicable Level 1 clause or safeguarding obligation based on the Phase II headline.

Does Onyx know when Phase II will restart?

This page makes no restart-date claim. Consult current official notices; do not use a marketing page as the authority for a future deadline.

Can Onyx tell us our contract is legally compliant?

No. We provide defined readiness support, not a legal opinion, certification or guarantee of government acceptance.
