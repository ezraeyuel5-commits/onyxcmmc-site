Before you start: access and roles

Use the official SPRS CMMC page for current access instructions, entry guides and tutorials. It links separate resources for assessment entry and for the affirming official. Interface labels and permissions can change; follow the current official workflow rather than old third-party screenshots.

Your authorized users should use their own accounts and approved roles. Do not send Onyx passwords, security codes or session credentials. A screen-share should be limited to the task and information authorized for the engagement.

What to prepare for the record

The Level 1 results information specified in 32 CFR 170.15 includes the CMMC level, status date, assessment scope, associated CAGE codes and compliance result. Reconcile that information with the actual assessment before entry.

  • The assessed systems and associated CAGE information.
  • The accurate results and evidence supporting them.
  • The official’s identity, title, contact details and authority.
  • Any unresolved gaps that prevent a supported Final Level 1 result.
  • A record-retention owner and the next applicable assessment/affirmation date.

Do not confuse an entered assessment with a completed affirmation. Use the current SPRS guidance to verify the status shown after the authorized official completes the process.

Who makes the affirmation?

32 CFR 170.22 requires a senior-level representative from within the organization who is responsible for ensuring CMMC compliance and has authority to affirm continuing compliance. The affirmation identifies that official and states that the applicable requirements have been implemented and will be maintained within the assessment scope.

For Level 1, the affirmation is required at completion of the self-assessment and annually thereafter. Onyx may organize evidence and guide the process, but the client’s official reviews, makes and owns the affirmation.

Accuracy, changes and handoff

Do not affirm an unsupported result or treat a planned fix as an implemented safeguard. An internal gap list does not create a Level 1 exception. Your company remains responsible for maintaining the safeguards after a readiness engagement ends.

Keep the assessment evidence and relevant record under your company’s information-handling rules. When a prime requests proof, clarify exactly what record it needs and share only through an approved channel. If a requirement, scope or compliance condition changes, review the applicable rules and obtain qualified advice rather than assuming an old record remains sufficient.

Common questions

Can our outside consultant affirm as our company official?

The rule assigns that role to an authorized senior-level representative within the organization. Onyx prepares and guides; your official makes the affirmation.

Does Level 1 use a numerical SPRS score?

Level 1 records a compliance result based on the required safeguards; it is not the numerical NIST SP 800-171 assessment score. Ask what record is being requested.

Should I send my SPRS password to Onyx?

No. Use your own authorized account. Do not share credentials or authentication codes with Onyx.

Is a draft assessment the same as an affirmed status?

No. Verify completion of the separate affirmation process using current official SPRS guidance; do not assume data entry alone finished the task.
